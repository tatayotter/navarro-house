## Room

| Wall | Length | Other side | Condition and notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Wall 1 (firewall) | 418 cm | Outside, above neighbour's roof | Main heat source. Glass-block strip and small vent high up. Gets a double wall. Only route for aircon tubing. |
| Wall 2 | 367 cm | Neighbour's house | Window sealed from the neighbour's side and an old aircon hole, both to be bricked up. Rust-streaked cracks. |
| Wall 3 | 421 cm | Living room | Lower half concrete, upper half double wall. Door to be rehung. |
| Wall 4 | 367 cm | Neighbour's house | No openings. Water staining from the beam; cracks near the Wall 1 corner. |

The roof slab is waterproofed and covered by the rooftop roof, so the ceiling is not a major heat source. Ceiling about 239.5 cm; beam underside 218 cm.

## Layout

- **King bed (183 × 190 cm):** headboard on Wall 1, a 40 cm bedside table each side.
- **Bunk bed (2 teens):** 100 × 200 cm in the Wall 1 / Wall 4 corner, ladder facing the bed.
- **Wardrobe:** full height along Wall 3 for about 170 cm, then a built-in desk (100 cm wide) by the door, with a shelf above. Foot of the bed stays clear.
- **Aircon:** 1.5 HP inverter split, high on Wall 2 over the foot of the bed, blowing toward Wall 4, away from heads. Pipes out through the firewall.
- **Exhaust fan:** in the small vent on Wall 1 near Wall 4.
- **Door:** rehung to swing toward Wall 4 with a stop at 90°.
- **Floor:** self-levelling layer, then light-oak SPC vinyl.

**Ceiling and lighting:** drop ceiling at the beam underside (about 218 cm), hiding beams, slab damage and wiring. **Changed 19 Nov:** White Oak PVC ceiling panels (24 pieces, about ₱13.5k–19.7k installed, Online) instead of gypsum, about the same cost, so the total is unchanged. No ceiling painting needed. See the [ceilings page](../house/ceilings.html). 4 recessed downlights, 2 paper-lantern pendants over the bedside shelves, and hidden LED strips (under bed, shelves, wardrobe plinth, desk shelf, top bunk, fluted-wall cove).
