## Notes by section

- **Floor:** buy the bedroom and living room SPC in one order from the same batch so the shade matches. Laying the click-lock SPC yourself saves ₱2,900–5,700.
- **Walls:** a tinted warm greige costs more than white, so ask for the tinted price. The stairwell walls need scaffolding.
- **Ceiling (changed 19 Nov):** White Oak PVC panels, 34 pieces, instead of gypsum. Book one crew for the bedroom, living room, kitchen and dining together, so the height and panel direction match. Hold a sample against the walnut first. The hugger fan hangs from the slab. No cove lights (dropped 19 Nov). Details on the [ceilings page](../house/ceilings.html).
- **Electrical:** 3 circuits and about 19 points (8 lights, 2 switch panels, 9 sockets). Use one smart-home app for both rooms. The final layout is on the [electrical page](../house/electrical-layout-v1.html).
- **Carpentry:** the slat screen doubles as the safety barrier above the counter: slats fixed top and bottom with gaps under 10 cm. A ready-made TV console saves about ₱7,000–12,000.
- **Terrace slider:** the biggest single item. Get at least two local fabricator quotes with the same frame series, black powder-coat and glass thickness. Laminated glass for typhoons adds about ₱15,000–25,000.
