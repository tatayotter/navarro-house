**All sizes are provisional.** They come from the floor plans and the 3D model, not from a tape measure. Ask each supplier to measure on site before they cut anything, and use the table below to compare quotes on the same spec. All prices are online estimates (Sept–Nov 2026) until local quotes come in.

## Spec for all items (tell every supplier the same)

- **Frame:** aluminium, **black powder-coated** (matte). Ask for powder-coat, not black anodised, and get both prices if they differ.
- **Doors and tall panels:** **tempered** glass only (safety glass). Ask for the heavy-duty door series, not the lighter window series.
- **Windows:** 5–6 mm clear float glass unless noted. Frosted = sandblasted or acid-etched.
- **Seals:** rubber gaskets and brush seals all round; outside items get a raised sill track with weep holes.
- **Screens:** aluminium frame with fibreglass mesh, black.
- **Quote must include:** supply, delivery, installation, sealant, locks/handles, and say the warranty.

## Measure on site before ordering

General rule: measure each opening **after** it is finished (plastered, or the steel frame is in), not before.

- **Every opening:** measure width at the top, middle and bottom, and height at the left, middle and right. Use the smallest number. Measure both diagonals; if they differ by more than 1 cm, tell the supplier.
- **1. Jalousie:** the existing terrace window opening (width, height, sill height from the terrace floor). Check if the old frame comes out and who removes it. Confirm the 6 bays divide the width evenly.
- **2. Living slider:** only after the wall is widened and the new beam or lintel is in (engineer). Note the finished floor level (self-leveller + SPC) so the bottom track sits flush, and the ceiling height for the head track.
- **3. Car bay door:** after the welder builds the steel partition frame. Floor level on both sides, ceiling height (about 2.40 m), and the clearance to the wheel stop.
- **4. Rooftop slider:** after the half wall and steel frame are built. Finished tile level, and a raised sill (5–10 cm) so rain doesn't blow in. Faces the open slab and typhoon wind: ask the supplier which series they'd use there.
- **5. Awning window:** from the T&B floor to the sill (1.80 m) after the T&B floor is raised and tiled.
- **6. Shower screen:** after the wall and floor tiles are done; measure tiled wall to tiled wall.
- **7. Aircon sleeve:** buy the aircon first and use its sleeve size from the manual.
