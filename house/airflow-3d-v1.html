Gate faces southwest. Toggle doors and windows, switch the wind, and pick a time of day under Heat to compare setups. Blue dots are fresh air coming in, orange is warm air going out. Room temperatures are a simple heat-balance estimate from local monthly averages (±2–3 °C), not an engineering airflow (CFD) or thermal study.
