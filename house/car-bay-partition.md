## What changed (19 Nov)

The old plan was a steel frame with a 1.80 m sliding glass door. The new plan is a **solid wall** with two openings:

- a **solid wood main door** in the walkway beside the car, next to the water tank
- a **sliding window** over the car, so you can watch the car from the dining side and slide it open for air

## Layout (from the left wall, looking from the dining side)

|---|---|---|---|
| Solid wall | 0 to 0.50 m | 0.50 m | Full height |
| Sliding window | 0.50 to 2.03 m (over the car) | 1.50 × 1.00 m opening | Sill 1.00 m, top 2.00 m |

- **The door swings into the dining side.** On the car side it would hit the tank or the car. It is **hinged on the window side** (decided 19 Nov), so the open door rests beside the window and the dining light switch and water valves on the right wall stay easy to reach.
- **Keep clear on the dining side:** the crossover valve and roof riser shut-off at 1.2 m on the side wall just inside the partition, and the dining light switch near them. The dining table should sit left of the door swing (about 0.9 m deep into the dining).
- **The 0.80 m door matches the front pedestrian door**, so anything that fits through the front fits through here. Bigger items come in through the car shutter.

## Door spec (carpentry)

- **Leaf:** solid wood panel door, 0.80 × 2.10 m, about 45 mm thick, kiln-dried mahogany or tanguile. Ask for kiln-dried, not air-dried, so it does not warp.
- **Jamb:** solid wood 2″ × 6″, treated against termites.
- **Finish:** PU or marine varnish on **all six sides**, including the top and bottom edges. The car side gets humidity and splash from washing the car.
- **Hardware:** 3 stainless 4″ hinges, keyed lever lockset plus a deadbolt (this is the house's inner security door), a floor door stop.
- **Threshold:** a raised granite or marble sill, 2–3 cm, so car-wash water stays in the car bay. The car bay floor should slope towards the front.

## Window spec (glass and aluminium)

- 2 panels, 1 fixed and 1 sliding, black powder-coated aluminium, **6 mm clear tempered glass** (no frosting), sliding fly screen on the dining side, latch on the dining side.
- It is listed as item 3 in the [glass and aluminium schedule](glass-aluminium.html), so quote it with the other windows.

## Wall

- 4″ hollow blocks (CHB), plastered and painted on both sides, about 7 m² after the openings.
- One concrete lintel over the door and the window, and a sill band under the window.
- **Check with the engineer:** the lintel size and how the new wall ties into the two side walls (they are shared with the neighbours).

## Airflow

| Open | Open area | Compared to the old slider |
|---|---|---|
| Door and window | about 2.2 m² | about 15% more |
| Door only | about 1.5 m² | about 20% less |
| Window only (door locked) | about 0.7 m² | about a third |

- The window lets the car bay breathe even when the door is locked, for example at night.
- **Close both the door and the window while the car engine runs**, so exhaust stays out of the house.
